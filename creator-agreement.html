📜 Creator Agreement, AUP & Vendor Policy

Version 1 · Last updated: September 2026

ℹ️ You're viewing this in a regular browser. To accept the agreement, open it from the bot on Telegram while completing your creator application.

1.Independent Contractor Status

  • Approved creators operate as independent contractors, not employees or agents of the Platform. Creators retain full intellectual property ownership while granting the Platform a non-exclusive license to store, display, tag, and distribute their content through the bot interface and backend storage architecture.

2.Revenue Splits & Payouts

  • The Platform automatically deducts its designated platform commission from every successful sale before crediting the creator's balance.
  • Payouts are processed exclusively via cryptocurrency (e.g., USDT) to the wallet address provided by the creator, subject to minimum withdrawal thresholds and network transaction fees.

3.Content Warranties & Vetting

  • Creators warrant that they own all rights to uploaded content or possess legal authorization to distribute it.
  • All individuals depicted must be consenting adults (18+). Valid age-verification documents and identity checks must be provided during the application process.

4.Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) & Prohibited Content

The uploading, sharing, or selling of the following content is strictly prohibited:

  • Non-consensual media (NCII), deepfakes, or stolen content belonging to third parties.
  • Content depicting minors (under 18) or any form of underage exploitation.
  • Extreme violence, illegal substances, malware, or unauthorized external promotional links.

Enforcement: Violations will result in immediate content removal, dashboard suspension, and the forfeiture of any unpaid earnings or balances.

5.Privacy Policy & Data Handling (Creators)

  • During onboarding, we collect real names, contact numbers, social handles, channel links, government-issued IDs, and verification videos to confirm identity and age.
  • These sensitive documents are stored with strict confidentiality and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized access.

6.Termination

  • The Platform reserves the right to suspend or terminate any creator account, freeze pending balances, and remove content immediately upon a breach of these terms.
